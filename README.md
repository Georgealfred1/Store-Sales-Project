# Store-Sales-Project

## 1. Project Name
Store-Sales-Project (Superstore Sales Database & Interactive Power BI Dashboards)

## 2. Project Idea
An end-to-end data analysis and business intelligence project based on the Superstore dataset. The project encompasses raw data cleaning, relational database creation, Power BI data modeling, custom DAX measures, and designing four specialized Power BI dashboards covering executive overview, order fulfillment & shipping duration, product performance, and customer analysis to support data-driven retail decisions.

## 4. Work Plan
1. **Data Cleaning & Preparation:** Cleaning raw Superstore records, handling missing values, and standardizing data formats.
2. **Database Creation:** Structuring and storing the cleaned data into a relational database.
3. **Data Modeling & DAX Measures:** Building table relationships in Power BI and writing custom DAX measures for core KPIs and shipping duration metrics.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Designing four dedicated Power BI dashboards (Overview, Order Fulfillment Duration, Product Analysis, and Customer Analysis).
5. **Review & Finalization:** Validating metrics across all dashboards and ensuring consistent layout and interactivity.

## 6. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Sales & Net Profit:** Overall revenue generated and profit margins across regions and time periods.
* **Order Fulfillment & Shipping Duration:** Average, minimum, and maximum days required to process and ship orders.
* **Product Category Profitability:** Sales volume, revenue, and profit contribution per category and sub-category.
* **Customer Segmentation & Retention:** Order frequency, average order value, and revenue share per customer segment.
* **Total Orders & Quantity Sold:** Total number of transactions and units sold.
